$$\mathbf{\text{Formal Charge} = V - (\text{Dots} + \text{Lines})}$$

- **$V$** = Group valence electrons (from the Periodic Table: $\text{C}=4, \text{N}=5, \text{O}=6, \text{Halogens}=7$)
    
- **$\text{Dots}$** = Total count of unshared individual electrons (not pairs)
    
- **$\text{Lines}$** = Total number of bond sticks attached to that atom
- 
**Quick Drill ($O_3$ from NCERT):**

![[Pasted image 20260929163007.png]]

1. **Central O (1):** $6 - (2\text{ dots} + 3\text{ lines}) = \mathbf{+1}$
    
2. **Double-bonded O (2):** $6 - (4\text{ dots} + 2\text{ lines}) = \mathbf{0}$
    
3. **Single-bonded O (3):** $6 - (6\text{ dots} + 1\text{ line}) = \mathbf{-1}$
    
    _Check:_ $+1 + 0 + (-1) = 0$ (matches the neutral charge of $O_3$).
