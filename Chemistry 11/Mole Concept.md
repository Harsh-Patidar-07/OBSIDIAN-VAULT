## Important concept

- Molar mass of $\text{NO}_3^- = 14 + 3(16) = 62\text{ g/mol}$.
    
- Moles of $\text{NO}_3^- = \frac{3.1 \times 10^{-3}\text{ g}}{62\text{ g/mol}} = 5 \times 10^{-5}\text{ mol}$.
    
- Total electrons per ion $= 7 (\text{N}) + 3 \times 8 (\text{O}) + 1 (\text{charge}) = 32\text{ electrons}$.
    
- Total electrons $= 5 \times 10^{-5} \times 32 \times 6.022 \times 10^{23} \approx 9.63 \times 10^{20}$.
