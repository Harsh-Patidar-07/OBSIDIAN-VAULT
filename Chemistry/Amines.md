> Why **Aniline** is a weaker base than **cyclohexylamine**
> Reason : 
> - In Aniline, the lone pair of electrons of nitrogen atom are involved in resonance hence **less**, are available for donation. There is no such effect in cyclohexylamine ; hence it is a stronger base

---

> **Trimethylamine** reacts with *BF3* while **Triphenylamine** doesn't
> Reason :
> - Trimethylamine has 3 methyl groups with (+I) effect which increases the electron density on nitrogen and hence it is a **lewis base** and reacts with **Lewis acid : BF3**. On the other hand, in **triphenylamine**, the presence of three phenyl groups have (-I) effect that decreases the electron density on nitrogen making it **less basic**
