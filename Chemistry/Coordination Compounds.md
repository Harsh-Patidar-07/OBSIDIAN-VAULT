### Secondary Valency 

According to Werner's coordination theory:

- **Primary valency** corresponds to the oxidation state of the central metal ion.
    
- **Secondary valency** corresponds to the **coordination number** of the central metal ion (the total number of sigma bonds formed between the ligands and the metal ion).
    

In the complex ion $[Co(NH_3)_5(NO_2)]^{2+}$:

- $NH_3$ (ammine) is a **monodentate** ligand, and there are 5 of them.
    
- $NO_2^-$ (nitrito-N) is also a **monodentate** ligand, and there is 1 of them.
    

$$\text{Secondary Valency} = \text{Total number of monodentate ligands} = 5 + 1 = 6$$

Therefore, the secondary valency of Cobalt ($Co$) in this complex is **6**.
