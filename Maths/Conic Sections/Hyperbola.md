# Hyperbola — BITSAT Formula Sheet

## 1. Standard Equation and Basic Properties

### 1.1 Horizontal Transverse Axis

**Standard equation:**

$$\boxed{\frac{x^2}{a^2}-\frac{y^2}{b^2}=1}$$

- Centre: $(0,0)$
    
- Vertices: $(\pm a,0)$
    
- Foci: $(\pm c,0)$
    
- Fundamental relationship: $\boxed{c^2=a^2+b^2}$
    
- Eccentricity: $\boxed{e=\frac ca>1}$
    
- Directrices: $\boxed{x=\pm\frac ae}$
    
- Length of transverse axis: $2a$
    
- Length of conjugate axis: $2b$
    
- Length of latus rectum: $\boxed{\frac{2b^2}{a}}$
    
- Asymptotes: $\boxed{y=\pm\frac ba x}$
    

### 1.2 Vertical Transverse Axis

**Standard equation:**

$$\boxed{\frac{y^2}{a^2}-\frac{x^2}{b^2}=1}$$

- Centre: $(0,0)$
    
- Vertices: $(0,\pm a)$
    
- Foci: $(0,\pm c)$
    
- Relationship: $c^2=a^2+b^2$
    
- Eccentricity: $e=c/a$
    
- Directrices: $y=\pm a/e$
    
- Length of latus rectum: $2b^2/a$
    
- Asymptotes: $y=\pm\frac ab x$
    

### 1.3 Shifted Hyperbola

For a horizontal transverse axis:

$$\boxed{\frac{(x-h)^2}{a^2}-\frac{(y-k)^2}{b^2}=1}$$

- Centre: $(h,k)$
    
- Vertices: $(h\pm a,k)$
    
- Foci: $(h\pm c,k)$
    
- Asymptotes: $\boxed{y-k=\pm\frac ba(x-h)}$
    

For a vertical transverse axis:

$$\boxed{\frac{(y-k)^2}{a^2}-\frac{(x-h)^2}{b^2}=1}$$

- Centre: $(h,k)$
    
- Vertices: $(h,k\pm a)$
    
- Foci: $(h,k\pm c)$
    
- Asymptotes: $\boxed{y-k=\pm\frac ab(x-h)}$

## 2. Tangents

### 2.1 Tangent at a Point

For the horizontal hyperbola, at $(x_1,y_1)$:

$$\boxed{\frac{xx_1}{a^2}-\frac{yy_1}{b^2}=1}$$

For the vertical hyperbola, at $(x_1,y_1)$:

$$\boxed{\frac{yy_1}{a^2}-\frac{xx_1}{b^2}=1}$$

The given point must lie on the hyperbola.

### 2.2 Tangent in Parametric Form

For the horizontal hyperbola, a parametric point is

$$P=(a\sec\theta,b\tan\theta)$$

The tangent is

$$\boxed{\frac{x\sec\theta}{a}-\frac{y\tan\theta}{b}=1}$$

For the vertical hyperbola, a parametric point is

$$P=(b\tan\theta,a\sec\theta)$$

The tangent is

$$\boxed{\frac{y\sec\theta}{a}-\frac{x\tan\theta}{b}=1}$$

### 2.3 Tangent with a Given Slope

For the horizontal hyperbola:

$$\boxed{y=mx\pm\sqrt{a^2m^2-b^2}}$$

Real tangents of this slope exist when

$$\boxed{a^2m^2\ge b^2}$$

For the vertical hyperbola:

$$\boxed{y=mx\pm\sqrt{a^2-b^2m^2}}$$

Real tangents of this slope exist when

$$\boxed{b^2m^2\le a^2}$$

### 2.4 Condition for a Line to Be Tangent

For the horizontal hyperbola, the line

$$lx+my+n=0$$

is tangent when

$$\boxed{n^2=a^2l^2-b^2m^2}$$

For the vertical hyperbola, the condition is

$$\boxed{n^2=a^2m^2-b^2l^2}$$

## 3. Minimum Practice Checklist

For BITSAT, prioritise these:

1. Identify the centre, vertices, foci and eccentricity.
    
2. Find the asymptotes and directrices.
    
3. Calculate the length of the latus rectum.
    
4. Find the tangent at a given point.
    
5. Find the tangent with a given slope.
    
6. Apply the tangent condition to a given line.
    

**Low priority for your current plan:** normals, complicated chord and midpoint-chord problems, director circle, pole-polar and elaborate special cases.

**Strategy:** Learn the boxed formulas, solve a handful of standard MCQs, and move on to complex numbers. Don't let hyperbola consume the time you've allocated to finishing Class 11 Maths.
