### Internal division

Suppose P divides AB internally in the ratio:

$$AP:PB=m:n$$

The coordinates of P are:

$$\boxed{P=\left(\frac{mx_2+nx_1}{m+n},\frac{my_2+ny_1}{m+n}\right)}$$



---

### External division

Now suppose P divides the line externally.

This means P lies outside the segment AB, on the extended line.

The formula becomes:


$$\boxed{P=\left(\frac{mx_2-nx_1}{m-n},\frac{my_2-ny_1}{m-n}\right)}$$


---


## Centroid of a triangle

$$\boxed{G=\left(\frac{x_1+x_2+x_3}{3},\frac{y_1+y_2+y_3}{3}\right)}$$

## Area of a triangle using coordinates

Very important

This is another extremely useful formula.

Suppose the three vertices of a triangle are:

$$ A=(x_1,y_1) $$

$$B=(x_2,y_2) $$

$$ C=(x_3,y_3) $$

The area of the triangle is:

$$\boxed{\Delta=\frac12\left|x_1(y_2-y_3)+x_2(y_3-y_1)+x_3(y_1-y_2)\right|}$$
