Coordinates of the vertices of a triangle $ABC$ are $A(12, 8)$, $B(-2, 6)$, and $C(6, 0)$.

- **Distance Formula:** $d = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}$
    
- **Centroid Formula:** $G = \left(\frac{x_1 + x_2 + x_3}{3}, \frac{y_1 + y_2 + y_3}{3}\right)$
    

1. **Classify the triangle by side lengths:**
    
    $$AB^2 = (-2 - 12)^2 + (6 - 8)^2 = (-14)^2 + (-2)^2 = 196 + 4 = 200$$
    
    $$BC^2 = (6 - (-2))^2 + (0 - 6)^2 = 8^2 + (-6)^2 = 64 + 36 = 100$$
    
    $$CA^2 = (12 - 6)^2 + (8 - 0)^2 = 6^2 + 8^2 = 36 + 64 = 100$$
    
    Because $BC^2 = CA^2 = 100$, the triangle is isosceles ($BC = CA = 10$). Also, $BC^2 + CA^2 = 100 + 100 = 200 = AB^2$. By the converse of Pythagoras' Theorem, $\triangle ABC$ is a **right-angled isosceles triangle**, with the right angle at vertex $C(6, 0)$. This rules out options (A), (B), and (C).


1. **Evaluate Option (D):**
    
    - **Orthocenter ($H$):** In any right-angled triangle, the orthocenter coincides with the vertex of the right angle.
        
        $$H = C = (6, 0) \implies x_H = 6$$
        
    - **Circumcenter ($O$):** In a right-angled triangle, the circumcenter is the midpoint of the hypotenuse $AB$.
        
        $$O = \left(\frac{12 + (-2)}{2}, \frac{8 + 6}{2}\right) = (5, 7) \implies x_O = 5$$
    - **Centroid ($G$):**
        $$x_G = \frac{12 + (-2) + 6}{3} = \frac{16}{3}$$
    - **Product of abscissas (x-coordinates):**

$$x_G \cdot x_H \cdot x_O = \frac{16}{3} \times 6 \times 5 = 16 \times 2 \times 5 = 160$$

**Correct Option:** **(D)**
