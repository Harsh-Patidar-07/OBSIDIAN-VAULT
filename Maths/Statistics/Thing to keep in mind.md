In any symmetric sequence (like an Arithmetic Progression), the mean is simply the average of the first and last terms:

$$\text{Mean} = \frac{\text{First Term} + \text{Last Term}}{2}$$


---

### Mean of 9 terms is 15. If one new term is added, mean becomes 16. Value of new term?

- **Standard Formula:**
 $$\text{Sum}_{\text{old}} = 9 \times 15 = 135$$
    $$\text{Sum}_{\text{new}} = 10 \times 16 = 160$$
    $$\text{New Term} = \text{Sum}_{\text{new}} - \text{Sum}_{\text{old}} = 160 - 135 = 25$$

**Intuition (Deviation method):** The incoming 10th term must supply its own new average ($16$) plus contribute $+1$ to each of the previous $9$ terms to raise their baseline from $15$ to $16$:
$$\text{New Term} = 16 + (9 \times 1) = 25$$
