**Standard Formulas:**

- Escape Velocity: $v_e = \sqrt{\frac{2GM}{R_e}}$
    
- Gravitational Potential Energy: $U(r) = -\frac{GMm}{r}$

*When working in big distances and stuff, use Gravitational Potential energy, not " mgh "*
