Total mechanical energy in a circular orbit of radius $r$:

$$E_f = -\frac{GMm}{2r}$$

**Fact to Remember:** In any orbit problem, you don't need complex orbital mechanics to classify the path shape. Just check the speed against $v_0$ and $v_e$:

- $v = v_0 \implies$ Circular ($E < 0$)
    
- $v_0 < v < v_e \implies$ Elliptical ($E < 0$)
    
- $v = v_e \implies$ Parabolic ($E = 0$)
    
- $v > v_e \implies$ Hyperbolic / Unbound ($E > 0$)

### **The Cheat Sheet: The Only 3 Potentials You Ever Need**

In all of JEE/BITSAT gravitation and electrostatics, there are really only three geometries:

|**Object**|**Point of Interest**|**Potential V**|**Why it's easy**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Point Mass / Hollow Shell (outside)**|Distance $r$|$-\frac{GM}{r}$|Base definition|
|**Ring**|Axis at distance $x$|$-\frac{GM}{\sqrt{r^2 + x^2}}$|Every point on ring is at distance $\sqrt{r^2+x^2}$|
|**Solid Sphere (inside)**|Center ($r=0$)|$-\frac{3}{2}\frac{GM}{R}$|$1.5\times$ deeper than the surface|
