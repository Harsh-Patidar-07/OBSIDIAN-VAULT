## Steady state in heat transfer

The temperature at any fixed point of a body does not change with time, even though heat may continuously flow through it.

Mathematically:

$$\boxed{\frac{\partial T}{\partial t}=0}$$​

This means temperature is not changing with time at a fixed position.

Notice something VERY important: this does not mean the temperature is the same everywhere.

The left end can be $100C$, the middle $50∘C$, and the right end $0∘C$.
