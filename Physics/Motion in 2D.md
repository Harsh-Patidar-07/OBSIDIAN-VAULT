# Inclined Plane

> $$R = \sqrt{X^2 + Y^2}$$
> $$R_{\text{up}} = \frac{2u^2 \sin\alpha \cos(\alpha + \theta)}{g \cos^2\theta}$$
> 
> $$R_{\text{down}} = \frac{2u^2 \sin\alpha \cos(\alpha - \theta)}{g \cos^2\theta} = \frac{u^2}{g \cos^2\theta} \left[ \sin(2\alpha - \theta) + \sin\theta \right]$$
> 
> $$T = \frac{2u \sin\alpha}{g \cos\theta}$$

## Maximum Height on an Inclined Plane

* **$\theta$**: Incline angle with the horizontal
* **$\alpha$**: Angle of projection relative to the inclined plane
* **$u$**: Initial projection speed

---

### 1. Max Height Perpendicular to Incline ($H_{\perp}$)
Maximum distance measured perpendicularly from the inclined plane surface:

$$H_{\perp} = \frac{u_\perp^2}{2 g_\perp} = \frac{u^2 \sin^2\alpha}{2g \cos\theta}$$

---

### 2. Max Absolute Vertical Height from Launch Point ($H_{\text{vert}}$)
Maximum vertical height reached above the point of projection:

$$H_{\text{vert}} = \frac{u^2 \sin^2(\alpha + \theta)}{2g}$$

---

### 3. Max Vertical Clearance Above Incline ($H_y'$)
Vertical distance from the surface of the incline up to the trajectory peak:

$$H_y' = \frac{u^2 \sin^2\alpha}{2g \cos^2\theta}$$
