##### The Big Picture Rule

Whenever you multiply dimensions that expand independently:

- **Area ($2\text{D}$):** Sum the linear expansion coefficients of the 2 orthogonal edges:
    
    $$\beta = \alpha_x + \alpha_y$$
    
- **Volume ($3\text{D}$):** Sum the linear expansion coefficients of all 3 orthogonal edges:
    
    $$\gamma = \alpha_x + \alpha_y + \alpha_z = \beta_{xy} + \alpha_z$$
