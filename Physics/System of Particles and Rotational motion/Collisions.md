# 12. Coefficient of Restitution ($e$)

$$e = \frac{\text{Velocity of separation of point of contact}}{\text{Velocity of approach of point of contact}}$$

> [!NOTE] 
> Velocities are taken strictly **along the Line of Impact (LOI)**.

---

### Formula

For two colliding bodies with velocities along the line of impact:
* **Before Collision:** Speeds $u_1$ and $u_2$ with $u_1 > u_2$ (approaching)
* **During Collision:** Normal contact force pairs $N$ act along the line of impact
* **After Collision:** Speeds $v_1$ and $v_2$ with $v_2 > v_1$ (separating)

$$e = \frac{v_2 - v_1}{u_1 - u_2}$$

---

### Classification of Collisions (on basis of KE loss)

| Collision Type | Value of $e$ | Kinetic Energy Conservation |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Elastic** | $e = 1$ | KE is conserved ($\Delta K = 0$) |
| **Partially Elastic** / **Inelastic** | $0 < e < 1$ | Partial loss of KE |
| **Perfectively Inelastic** | $e = 0$ | Maximum loss of KE (bodies stick / move with same velocity along LOI) |

## Rocket ( Variable Mass  concept )

- **Thrust Force on a Rocket:**
    
    $$F_{\text{thrust}} = v_{\text{rel}} \left\vert{} \frac{dm}{dt} \right\vert{}$$
    
- **Rocket Velocity Equation (Tsiolkovsky):**
    
    $$v = u + v_{\text{rel}} \ln\left(\frac{m_0}{m}\right) - gt$$
    
    _(If gravity is neglected, drop the $gt$ term)._
