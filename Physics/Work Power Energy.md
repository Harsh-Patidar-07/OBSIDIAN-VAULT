| Feature | $W_{\text{all}} = \Delta K$ | $W_{\text{nc, ext}} = \Delta K + \Delta U$ |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Gravity accounted as** | Work ($W_g = -mg\Delta h$) | Potential Energy ($U_g = mgh$) |
| **Springs accounted as** | Work ($W_s = -\Delta(\frac{1}{2}kx^2)$) | Potential Energy ($U_s = \frac{1}{2}kx^2$) |
| **Friction / External push** | Included in $W_{\text{all}}$ | Included in $W_{\text{nc, ext}}$ |
| **Right-hand side** | Only $\frac{1}{2}mv^2$ terms | Both $\frac{1}{2}mv^2$ and $mgh / \frac{1}{2}kx^2$ terms |
